Privacy Policy

Last updated: · Template: have a lawyer review before you rely on it.

This page explains, in plain language, what Viora does with your information.

What stays on your device

  • Your chats, starred messages, agents, notebooks and settings.
  • Memory: lasting facts and short notes about past chats. You can view, delete or turn this off in Settings.

What is sent to AI providers

When you send a message, the text (and any files, pages or images you attach) is sent to the AI model that answers it. When Viora searches the web, the search terms and the pages it reads are fetched on your behalf.

Accounts and backup

An account is optional. If you create one, we store your email and a salted hash of your password, never the password itself. If you turn on cloud backup, your chats, agents and settings are uploaded to your private backup. Personal API keys are never uploaded.

Page automation

Automate reads the page you are on only while you run a task. It asks before risky actions such as buying, sending or deleting.

Your choices

  • Delete any chat, memory note or agent at any time.
  • Sign out or delete your account from the account page.
  • Uninstall the extension to remove all on-device data.
