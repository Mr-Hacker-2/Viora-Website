Privacy Policy

Last updated: · Template: have a lawyer review before you rely on it.

This page explains, in plain language, what Viora does with your information.

What stays on your device

  • Your chats, starred messages, agents, notebooks and settings.
  • Memory: lasting facts and short notes about past chats. You can view, delete or turn this off in Settings.

What is sent to AI providers

When you send a message, the text (and any files, pages or images you attach) is sent to the AI model that answers it. When Viora searches the web, the search terms and the pages it reads are fetched on your behalf.

Accounts and backup

An account is optional. If you create one, we receive your name, email and profile picture from Google, and never your password. If you turn on cloud backup, your chats, agents and settings are uploaded to your private backup. Personal API keys are never uploaded.

Your IP address and location

  • IP address. When you sign in or send a message, we record the IP address you connect from. We keep the latest one and up to nine earlier ones, with the first and last time each was seen, on your account record.
  • Location, only if you share it. If a question depends on where you are, Viora asks first, and then your browser asks for permission. If you allow it, your exact location (latitude and longitude) is stored on your account and on this device, and is sent along with your messages. That means it also reaches the AI model provider as part of the request, so the AI can use it.
  • Who can see it. Viora’s administrator can see your IP addresses and shared location in the admin dashboard. Each time that list is viewed, it is written to an audit log.
  • Why. To keep the service secure, to investigate abuse, and to respond to valid requests from law enforcement or other authorities, or to protect people’s safety. We may disclose this information for those purposes.
  • How long. Until you clear your location, or the account record is removed. IP addresses stay on the account record.

Personal Intelligence Discovery (optional)

Personal Intelligence Discovery is an optional feature available only to people who confirm they are at least 18 and verify ownership of their email. It is off by default. If you opt in, Viora uses permitted public-source APIs and search services to look for public information that may be associated with your verified email. Depending on the configured providers, the email address or a one-way hash of it may be sent to GitHub, Gravatar, or Brave Search. A provider may return unrelated or mistaken matches. Searches run in the background on Viora's server, so they continue if you close the page; the first search starts automatically once your email is verified and you have opted in, and you can refresh, pause, or turn discovery off at any time. Pausing or turning discovery off cancels any queued or running search, and deleting your account cancels and deletes its queued searches. A temporary provider failure is retried automatically, and a failed search never changes your existing profile.

Viora does not sign in to private accounts, read inboxes or private messages, use leaked databases, bypass access controls, or automatically scrape social networks. Findings include source links and a confidence label, and remain pending until you approve them. You can reject, correct, or delete findings. Only approved profile details are stored in your private account storage, and only details relevant to a chat may be sent to the AI provider with that request. Personal Intelligence is not supplied to Humanizer. Turning discovery off stops future searches and AI use of the profile; deleting the profile permanently removes its stored object versions. Your account remains active.

Your choices

  • Say “Not now” when Viora asks for your location, or block location for this site in your browser settings. Viora still works, and you can type your city or area instead.
  • Clear a location you shared with “Clear shared location” on the account page.
  • Delete any chat, memory note or agent at any time.
  • Sign out or delete your account from the account page.
  • Uninstall the extension to remove all on-device data.
