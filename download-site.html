From your phone to your computer

1

Download on your phone

Tap Download website ZIP. If asked, choose Download or Save. On iPhone, check Files → Downloads; on Android, check Files → Downloads.

2

Transfer the ZIP

Move the ZIP to your PC using a USB cable, cloud storage (such as OneDrive or Google Drive), or another file-transfer method you trust.

3

Extract it on the PC

In Windows File Explorer, right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All…. Open the extracted folder to view the website files.

Want to publish changes? Editing the downloaded files on your PC does not automatically change the live website. Commit and push the changes to the Viora-Website GitHub repository, then allow the hosting service to redeploy.
