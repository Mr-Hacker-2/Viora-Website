Turn on discovery

When you turn this on, Viora's server searches these public sources for only after your separate adult confirmation and opt-in:

    Viora never signs in to your accounts, asks for other passwords, reads private messages or inboxes, uses leaked databases or gets around a site's protections. Results are stored privately on your account, aren't shown to anyone else, and stay "pending" until you approve them. You can turn discovery off or permanently delete your profile at any time. Search results are not automatically treated as yours unless you separately enable automatic approval for strong matches. Possible and uncertain matches always wait for your review.

    Verify your email first

    Discovery is only available once you've proven you own your email, so nobody can look up someone else's address. Sign in with Google using this email to verify it.

    What Viora uses in AI conversations

    Only details you approved (and strong matches, if you turned on automatic approval) can be used, and only the few relevant to what you are asking. They go to the AI as background, never as instructions, and never to the Humanizer. Pending, rejected and uncertain findings are never used. Pausing keeps your approved details in use; turning discovery off stops all use. Searches run on Viora's server, so you can close this page while one is running.
