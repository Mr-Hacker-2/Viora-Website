Turn on discovery

When you turn this on, Viora's server searches these public sources for only after your separate adult confirmation and opt-in:

    Viora never signs in to your accounts, asks for other passwords, reads private messages or inboxes, uses leaked databases or gets around a site's protections. Results are stored privately on your account, aren't shown to anyone else, and stay "pending" until you approve them. You can turn discovery off or permanently delete your profile at any time. Search results are not automatically treated as yours.

    Verify your email first

    Discovery is only available once you've proven you own your email, so nobody can look up someone else's address. Sign in with Google using this email to verify it.
